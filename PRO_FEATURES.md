## Pro Features

### 1. Multiple Repositories

Save articles to different Git repositories with a single click. Perfect for organizing work by project, topic, or team.

**Use cases:**
- Maintain separate repositories for different projects (Research, Work, Personal)
- Organize articles by topic (Technology, Design, Business)
- Collaborate with teams by saving to shared repositories
- Archive old repositories while keeping your main repository clean

### 2. Custom Save Paths

Take control of how your articles are organized. Instead of the default `articles/YYYY/MM/` structure, define custom folder paths for each repository.

**Examples:**
- `research/2025/` for research articles
- `bookmarks/` for general reading
- `documentation/api-reference/` for technical documentation
- `team-notes/project-name/` for project-specific notes

### 3. Repository Labels

Give your repositories meaningful names for instant recognition. Switch between repositories without memorizing owner/repo combinations.

**Benefits:**
- Quick identification of repositories at a glance
- Easier navigation when managing multiple saves
- Better team communication (use descriptive labels like "Client Work" or "Research")
- Cleaner, more intuitive UI

### 4. Advanced File Organization

Automatically organize your articles with date-based folder structures (YYYY/MM) and include timestamps in filenames to prevent collisions.

**Organization options:**
- **Date-based folders**: Automatically organize by year/month
- **Timestamps in filenames**: Prevent duplicate filenames when saving multiple articles at once
- **Custom paths**: Combine with custom save paths for maximum control
- **Consistent structure**: Keep your repository organized and easy to navigate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### License & Activation

**How do I activate my Pro license?**

After purchasing, you'll receive a license key via email. Go to the extension settings, scroll to "Pro License", paste your key, and click "Activate". Your Pro features will be immediately available.

**Can I use my license on multiple devices?**

Yes! Your license key works on any device where you install the extension. You can use My Stash Mark Pro on your desktop, laptop, and other computers.

**How long does my license last?**

For one-time purchases, your license never expires. For subscriptions, you'll have access as long as your subscription is active.

**Can I transfer my license to someone else?**

Yes, you can deactivate your license and share the key with someone else. However, only one person can use the key at a time.

### Features & Usage

**Do I need Pro to save articles?**

No, the core feature is free. Pro features are only for advanced organization and multi-repository support.

**Can I downgrade from Pro to Free?**

Yes, you can deactivate your license anytime. Your existing articles remain saved—you'll just lose access to Pro features like multiple repositories.

**What happens if my subscription expires?**

You'll lose access to Pro features, but all your saved articles remain in your repositories. You can reactivate anytime.

**Can I use Pro with both GitHub and GitLab?**

Yes! Each repository can use a different Git provider. One repository can be GitHub, another can be GitLab.

### Technical Questions

**Is my data secure?**

Absolutely. Your articles are stored in your own Git repositories. We never have access to your data. Your Personal Access Token is stored only in your browser, never on our servers.

**How do I backup my Pro license key?**

Keep your license key safe (treat it like a password). If you lose it, you can request a recovery email from your purchase receipt.

**Does Pro work offline?**

No, My Stash Mark requires an internet connection to save articles to Git. However, articles you've already saved are available in your Git repositories anytime.
